About Eduyog

One brand, two focused verticals

Eduyog is the primary digital brand of Eduyog Techno Solution Pvt. Ltd. It is the central entry point for the company's two business verticals, Education and Fitness.

Each platform under Eduyog has its own clear purpose, audience and way of working, so students and businesses always know which service they are using.

  • Education

    Three independent platforms: Saritex for academic support, Eduyarp for professional training and technical classes, and StudentAQ for international student career support.

    See Education

  • Fitness

    A B2B-focused platform offering marketing, promotional and digital support to fitness and wellness businesses.

    See Fitness
